Our Business Segments

Petrochemical Operations

We manage the sourcing, logistics, and distribution of essential petrochemical products. We bridge the gap between heavy industrial needs and reliable supply chains, ensuring consistent delivery for manufacturing and energy partners across Nigeria and the region.

Regional Energy Logistics

With physical presence in Lagos, Abuja, and Akwa Ibom, we provide localized support for downstream oil and gas distribution, regional fuel and lubricant supply chains, and field operations infrastructure maintenance across Nigeria's key economic hubs.

Strategic Industrial Consulting

Leveraging the expertise of our visionary leadership team, FIPCO offers high-level advisory services for energy projects, regulatory compliance within Nigeria, and cross-border energy trade partnerships in West Africa.

Oil Exploration

FIPCO actively pursues upstream opportunities in Nigeria's prolific hydrocarbon basins. Our exploration strategies leverage advanced geological assessment, strategic partnerships with regulatory bodies, and deep knowledge of Nigeria's oil and gas licensing framework to identify and develop commercial opportunities.

About FIPCO

Our Story

First Interstate Petrochemicals Ltd. (FIPCO) emerged as an emerging leader in Nigeria's energy and industrial landscape. From our inception, we have focused on delivering sustainable energy solutions through innovation, integrity, and operational excellence.

Our team brings together visionary leaders with deep roots in Nigeria's economic and diplomatic sectors, combined with experts in energy logistics, petrochemical operations, and strategic consulting. This unique blend of experience positions us to address complex challenges in Nigeria's downstream energy sector.

Our Vision

We envision FIPCO as the trusted bridge between Nigeria's industrial growth needs and sustainable energy solutions. We aim to become the preferred partner for companies seeking reliable, innovative, and ethically managed petrochemical and energy logistics services across West Africa.

Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance, environmental responsibility, and safety protocols. Every decision we make reflects our core values of reliability, safety, and strategic partnership with our stakeholders.
